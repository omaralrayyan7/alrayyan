This page tells you what information Alrayyan Group collects when you use our website, how we use it, and what your rights are. By using alrayyangroup.online you agree to the terms below.

1. Who We Are

Alrayyan Group operates Alrayyan Tower — a premium office, retail and land rental business located on Queen Alia Street, Amman, Jordan. References to "we", "us" and "our" mean Alrayyan Group.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a visit-booking form or contact us, we collect the details you give us, which may include:

  • Your name, company, phone number, email address and WhatsApp number.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

4. Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored in your browser. We use only the cookies we need:

  • Essential cookies — remember your language preference (English / Arabic) and keep the booking form working.
  • Anonymous analytics — count page views so we can see which spaces visitors are most interested in. These cannot identify you personally.

When you first visit the site we show a banner asking you to accept or decline. If you decline you will be redirected away from the site, because the site cannot function without its essential cookies.
